Write for a reader who has not seen the project.

## 1. Write down what the reader has seen

Before the first sentence, write a list in your notes: what this reader has seen and the words they use. Take it from:

- the reader's own messages and documents,
- the letters and documents already sent to them,
- the product or pages they use.

When these sources do not settle a name, ask the user.

## 2. Read the project's rules for this reader

Read the project's memory files and project skills about writing for this reader. They hold the signature, the forms of address and the term tables. They win over this skill. A project skill for client PDFs, when one exists, wins over this skill.

## 3. Write

Use only the names on the step 1 list. Replace every other name with what the reader sees or does: a file, a column, a ticket code, a site nickname, an internal project name, an engineering metric.

Keep what the reader acts on: what changed for them, what they must do now, and each honest limit.

Write in the reader's language.

## Done

Every name in the text is on the step 1 list, or is replaced with what the reader sees or does.
